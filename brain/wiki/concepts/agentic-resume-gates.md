## Claim Language Guide

| Weak | Strong (focus on architecture) |
|------|-------------------------------|
| Prototyping diffusion models | Generative pipeline with evaluation gates and provenance tracking |
| Chatbot for leads | FSM agent with handoff logic and audit architecture |
| AI video editor | Multi-agent desk with clip provenance and formula validation |
| Built with LangChain | Custom orchestration with structured output validation |
| Scaled AI product | System with budget enforcement and usage tracking architecture |

## Writing Pattern

For each project describe:
1. **Architecture:** What components and how they connect
2. **Methodology:** How evaluation and quality are enforced
3. **Key design decisions:** What makes it robust
