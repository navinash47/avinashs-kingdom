# YouTube screenplay — layman craft (show, don’t tell)

Filed: 2026-08-11  
Venture: youtube-editor · dogfood `confirmation-bias-myside`

## Craft locks (human)

1. **Audience:** Layman + curious. Bias as a root of *ignorance* — not a dunk on stupidity.
2. **Show, don’t tell (Rajamouli):** Cold open = lived scene. No topic lecture, no naming the bias, no “is it stupidity?” before the scene lands.
3. **Arc:** Scene → recognition → curiosity question → *then* name mechanism → science → learning payoff.
4. **Term debut:** Font-montage flash + definition + background blur (`PLACEHOLDER_term_flash_*`). Graphics Desk / Manim later.
5. **Payoff:** Beautiful “new thing I learned” — never “people are stupid.”
6. **Timing:** Apply before Narration Desk (Phase 3).
